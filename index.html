WHO WE ARE

Proudly Tanzanian.
Built around hospitality.

Glory 1 Africa Trading Co. Limited is a hospitality-oriented company established by Tanzanians with a vision to create first-class hospitality services and supplies.

At Julius Nyerere International Airport, we operate a growing collection of retail, dining and passenger-service outlets across Terminal 1, Terminal 2 and Terminal 3, with a restaurant presence at Arusha Airport.
