Who we are

Glory 1 Africa Trading Co. Limited

Proudly Tanzanian, Glory 1 Africa Trading Co. Limited is a hospitality-oriented company established by Tanzanians with a vision to provide first-class hospitality services and supplies.

Our work focuses on making every customer experience welcoming through professional service, quality products and reliable operations. At Julius Nyerere International Airport, we serve travelers with care across our shops, restaurant and passenger services.
